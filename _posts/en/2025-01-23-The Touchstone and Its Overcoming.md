---
title: The Touchstone and Its Overcoming
locale: en
excerpt: >-
  The very act of testing gold against the touchstone, must be overcome
---

Faced with difficulties, challenges, and trials, one may at first remain undecided and evade them, turning a blind eye and a deaf ear. A different habit of mind, formed through rudimentary reflection, treats confronting difficulty as both a duty and a necessity, making difficulty a touchstone by which to establish value. Some go further: they see a test or challenge everywhere, forever bringing out the touchstone to reflect upon and examine themselves, as though founding a modern religion devoted to the development and manufacture of new forms of guilt.

Beyond these responses, those whose resolve is more authentic — and therefore wiser — have already taken the burden of difficulty upon themselves. They know that, at some point, difficulty as a touchstone, and the very act of testing gold against it, must be overcome, forgotten, and thereby transformed.  
Only then can they establish a just order of rank: the value attested by difficulty as a touchstone is fundamentally derivative and secondary. Ultimately, it is the will that evaluates difficulty — not difficulty that evaluates the will.
